# Replace this file

Copy this folder to `stories/<id>/`. The folder name and `book.json` `id` match. Keep `ready` false until the pages and pictures are real, then run `node tools/build-stories.js`. A story that is not ready never appears on the shelf.

Write pages in story order. The next card is the next page. There is no ranking, no For You, and no "because you read".

Each picture is its own JPEG, 864 by 1152, used on one page only. The whole figure sits inside the frame, head and feet included. The card shows that file whole.

Hindi is written as Hindi, not as English with the words swapped. Leave Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, and Malayalam out until you actually have them. A missing language stays English and the card says so.

If a scene is later tradition, say that in the sentence. Tell a grave scene gravely. Rama, when he appears, is a young clean-shaven prince. An older king may have a white beard. Do not draw a deity as a cartoon.
